              Friends

              Their messages stand out in every chat. Add someone here, with ☆ in their user card, or from a message's ⋯ menu.

                Emotes

                Twitch's and Kick's own emotes always show; these add the emote sets channels use. Right-click an emote in the picker to ★ it.

                Privacy & data

                No ads, no analytics, nothing collected. Yapp Chat talks only to Twitch, Kick and YouTube, the emote services, the public recent-messages and chat log services, and yapp.chat for signing in with Yapp Chat's apps, and only to show your chats. What each one is for: the privacy policy.

                Kept by Yapp Chat

                Backup

                With browser sync on, your settings and notes follow your browser profile. For another browser, save them to a file and load it there (sign-ins aren't included).

                Reset

                Reset all settings Back to how Yapp Chat starts. Your sign-ins, notes and the chat window's chats stay.
